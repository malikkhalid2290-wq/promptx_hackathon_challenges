Brand Voice Guide
Brand personality
AIS sounds like a brilliant senior student — knowledgeable but never condescending, excited about AI but grounded in real application. Think: a friend who just landed their first ML internship and can't stop sharing what they learned.
Tone mix by platform
InstagramEnergetic · Visual · Inspiring
LinkedInProfessional · Insightful · Proud
FacebookCommunity · Welcoming · Warm
✓ Do — write like this
"We built a sentiment analysis model in 2 hours. Here's what we learned." · "Your first AI project doesn't need to be perfect — it just needs to start." · "Applications close Friday. Your future self will thank you." · Use "we", "you", "let's" — AIS is a community.
✗ Don't — avoid this
Overly formal academic language. Jargon without explanation. Passive voice ("A workshop was held..."). Corporate buzzword soup. Bragging without substance. Posting in English only when Urdu adds warmth. Generic "Congratulations to our members!" without specifics.
Power keywords — always welcome

Visual Style Rules
Dark navy backgrounds (#0A1628) with Electric Blue and AI Green type/graphics — the signature AIS look.
Bold, single-idea posts. One insight, one stat, one question per graphic. No cluttered slides.
Tech aesthetic: circuit lines, neural net nodes, subtle grid overlays — never clipart or stock photos of laptops.
Real member photos — candid workshop shots, hackathon moments, whiteboard sessions. Authenticity over polish.
Consistent corner logo placement (top-right). AIS wordmark always in white on dark backgrounds.
AI-generated visuals for abstract concepts: neural networks, data flow, model architectures — generated via Midjourney/DALL-E.
No rainbow gradients, no neon unless intentional "cyber" theme. Stick to the 8-colour system.
No meme templates that undermine professional credibility. AIS memes are tech-specific and original.
No white backgrounds on posts — always dark or light blue-tinted. White feels generic and off-brand.
